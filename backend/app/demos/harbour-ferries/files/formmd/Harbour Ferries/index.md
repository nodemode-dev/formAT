# Harbour Ferries

Harbour Ferries is a made-up passenger ferry operator used as sample data. It runs five routes with eight vessels out of Town Pier, and this workspace holds its summer season, 1 May to 30 September 2026: 153 days of ridership and punctuality, the routes, the fleet, the fare mix and three earlier seasons for comparison.

## The season in numbers

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Riders | 350,698 |
| Average per day | 2,292 |
| Busiest day | 4,928 on Sun 12 Jul |
| Sailings run | 28,376 of 28,611 scheduled |
| On time | 95.4% of sailings run |
| Busiest route | Harbour Loop, 111,515 riders |
| Change on 2025 | +3.6% riders, -0.6 points on time |

## Databases

- [Daily ridership](Daily%20ridership.csv): one row per day with riders by route, weather, sailings and on-time counts
- [Routes](Routes.csv): the five routes with timetable, fare and season totals; each row opens a page about the route
- [Fleet](Fleet.csv): the eight vessels, where they work and when they are next surveyed
- [Fare mix](Fare%20mix.csv): riders by fare type
- [Season history](Season%20history.csv): 2023 to 2026 side by side
