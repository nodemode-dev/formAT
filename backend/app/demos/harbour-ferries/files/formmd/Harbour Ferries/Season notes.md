# Season notes

A short reading of the 2026 season. Every figure here is worked out from [Daily ridership](Daily%20ridership.csv).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Days worth remembering

- Busiest: Sun 12 Jul, 4,928 riders. Harbour Regatta day, clear weather.
- Quietest: Tue 19 May, 952 riders (rain).
- Hardest for punctuality: Mon 11 May, 85.4% on time with 9 sailings cancelled (fog).

## What stands out

- Weekends carry the season: Saturdays and Sundays average 2,987 riders against 2,012 on weekdays.
- Fog and wind cost punctuality, not rain: on-time running was 90.0% on foggy and windy days and 94.0% on rainy ones.
- [Routes](Routes.csv) shows how uneven the network is: Harbour Loop alone carried 31.8% of all riders.
- The relief vessel is listed in [Fleet](Fleet.csv); its survey falls due in November, after the season.
