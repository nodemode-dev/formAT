# Island Shuttle

The hourly boat to Gull Island: beaches, the bird reserve and one cafe. It is the route most sensitive to weather and the one that grows most at weekends.

## Timetable

- Stops: Town Pier, Gull Island
- First sailing 08:00, last sailing 19:00, every 60 minutes
- 35 minutes each way, 9.6 km
- 24 sailings a day in season

## Season 2026

- 64,714 riders, 18.5% of the network
- 17.6 riders per sailing on average
- Busiest day: Sun 12 Jul with 1,008 riders

## Vessels

- Fulmar: Sound class, built 2019, 148 seats, hybrid drive
- Shearwater: Sound class, built 2008, 140 seats, diesel drive
