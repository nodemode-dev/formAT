# Eastern Quay

A short commuter crossing to the workshops and housing on the east shore. Weekday mornings and evenings are the busy hours, so weekends lift it less than the leisure routes.

## Timetable

- Stops: Town Pier, Eastern Quay
- First sailing 06:45, last sailing 21:45, every 30 minutes
- 12 minutes each way, 1.9 km
- 62 sailings a day in season

## Season 2026

- 85,867 riders, 24.5% of the network
- 9.1 riders per sailing on average
- Busiest day: Sun 12 Jul with 1,281 riders

## Vessels

- Guillemot: Harbour class, built 2011, 80 seats, diesel drive
