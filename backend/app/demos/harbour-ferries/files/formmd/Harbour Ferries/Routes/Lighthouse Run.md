# Lighthouse Run

A longer open-water trip out to Skerry Light and back. Seven round trips a day in season; fog and strong wind cancel it before anything else.

## Timetable

- Stops: Marina, Skerry Light
- First sailing 09:00, last sailing 18:00, every 90 minutes
- 50 minutes each way, 14.8 km
- 14 sailings a day in season

## Season 2026

- 50,492 riders, 14.4% of the network
- 23.6 riders per sailing on average
- Busiest day: Sun 12 Jul with 807 riders

## Vessels

- Petrel: Sound class, built 2021, 148 seats, hybrid drive
