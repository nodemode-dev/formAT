# Harbour Loop

The circular service around the inner harbour. It runs every 20 minutes from early morning to late evening and carries more people than any other route, most of them short hops between Town Pier and the Marina.

## Timetable

- Stops: Town Pier, Fish Quay, Marina, North Mole
- First sailing 06:30, last sailing 22:30, every 20 minutes
- 25 minutes for the full loop, 4.2 km
- 49 sailings a day in season

## Season 2026

- 111,515 riders, 31.8% of the network
- 14.9 riders per sailing on average
- Busiest day: Sat 8 Aug with 1,461 riders

## Vessels

- Tern: Harbour class, built 2014, 96 seats, hybrid drive
- Kittiwake: Harbour class, built 2016, 96 seats, hybrid drive
