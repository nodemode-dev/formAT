# West Sands

The crossing from North Mole to the long beach at West Sands. Quiet in May, steady through the school holidays, and popular on clear evenings.

## Timetable

- Stops: North Mole, West Sands
- First sailing 07:30, last sailing 21:00, every 45 minutes
- 20 minutes each way, 5.1 km
- 38 sailings a day in season
