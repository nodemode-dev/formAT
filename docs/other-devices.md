# Other devices

FormAT runs on one computer, but you can carry on from your phone or another computer on the same network.

## Pairing

1. On the computer running FormAT, open Settings, Other devices, and choose Open to other devices.
2. Scan the code with your phone's camera, or open the link shown under it on the other device.
3. The other device opens FormAT, paired. It sees the same chats, live, both ways: start a chat on the computer and continue it on the phone.

A device that is not paired gets a page saying so. Forget paired devices unpairs every device at once (they pair again with the new code). Close to other devices stops FormAT listening on the network.

FormAT listens on your network (port 8492) only while this is on. The pairing link is a secret: anyone on your network who has it can use FormAT as you, with your API key. Share it only with your own devices.

## Share links

An artifact's Share button makes a read-only link to its latest version. While other devices are open the link uses your network address, so anyone on your network with the link can open that one artifact, without being paired. Stop sharing makes the link answer "no longer shared".

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the sidebar is a drawer: it starts closed, opens with the button at the top left, and closes when you pick a chat.
